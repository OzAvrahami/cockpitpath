# Phase 6 — Aircraft Systems

**Status:** ⬜ Planned

## Goal

Add conceptual aircraft-system learning that connects operational procedures to cockpit controls and underlying concepts.

## Why Now

Aircraft Systems becomes useful after the shared Procedure, Control, Hotspot, SystemComponent, and Concept relationships exist and users can return to their procedural or cockpit context.

## Deliverables

- Aircraft Systems.
- System Components.
- Component relationships.
- Conceptual diagrams.
- Learning sections.
- Concepts.
- Links to related Controls.
- Links to Guide Mode contexts.
- Links to Cockpit Explorer contexts.

Implementation details remain governed by the [Aircraft Systems UX](../ux/aircraft-systems.md) and its existing [design brief](../ai/claude-design/aircraft-systems/design-brief.md). System explanations and diagrams require the same source and verification discipline as procedures.

## Exit Criteria

- The vertical-slice AircraftSystem, SystemComponent, relationships, learning sections, and Concept render from published structured content.
- Conceptual diagrams are understandable and accessible.
- Related Control, Guide Mode, and Cockpit Explorer links preserve useful context in both directions.
- All technical content in the slice has required source and verification metadata.

## Dependencies

Phases 2, 4, and 5.
