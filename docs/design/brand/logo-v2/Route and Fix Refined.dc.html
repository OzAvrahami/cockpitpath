BRAND · LOGO · v2 · REFINEMENT OF CONCEPT 01
Route & Fix — Refined
Direction locked to Route & Fix; not re-explored. The concern with v1 was that a bent line with round dots reads as a generic maps pin. This pass replaces the plain circles with real aeronautical chart symbology — a solid fix triangle at the understood turn, an open fix triangle at the point ahead, and a short perpendicular reference tick at the origin, the kind of mark you would find on an approach chart. Fixed, upright orientation (not rotated to the route) keeps it reading as instrument notation rather than a route icon. Still two strokes and two shapes at 16px.
